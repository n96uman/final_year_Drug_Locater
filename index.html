How the system works

The Location-Based E-Pharmacy System links patients with licensed pharmacies in Hawassa City. You can discover who has your medicine in stock, compare regular and promotional prices, and review pharmacy locations before you order. Pharmacy teams use a separate dashboard to add medicines, track quantity and expiry, and keep listings current.
